Hustle. Tired players are slower and less accurate; fresh lines come on at each whistle.
Space
Tap for a wrist shot, hold for a slapshot. Press while a pass is coming to you for a one-timer. On defense: body check. Hustle into it for a bigger hit; pin someone near the boards to rattle the glass.
E
Pass toward the direction you're pushing. On defense: poke check, which sweeps your stick about five feet ahead, so line up with the puck. Poking from right behind the carrier lifts his stick. It also breaks up passes and shots.
Q
Tap to deke side to side. Pull back on the stick and tap for a toe drag. Tap while winding up a shot to fake it and make the goalie bite. Hold to shield the puck with your body. On defense: switch player.
